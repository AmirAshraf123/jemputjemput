Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Jemput is a website for designing wedding invitations, sending them to guests, and collecting their replies. It is run by the Jemput team. This page explains what information Jemput handles, why, who else processes it, and how to have it removed.

There are two kinds of people who use Jemput:

  • Hosts: people who sign in to make an invitation, usually the couple or their family.
  • Guests: people who receive an invitation link. Guests never create an account.

What we collect

From hosts

  • Sign-in details. Your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google also shares your name and profile picture with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Invitation content. What you type into an invitation, such as names, the date, time and venue, the message and dress code, plus any photo you upload and the design choices you make.
  • Templates you save.

About guests, entered by the host

  • The name on the invitation, an optional phone number, the number of seats offered, and which side of the family they are on.

From guests, when they reply

  • Whether they are coming, how many people, any dietary needs, and an optional note to the couple.

Dietary needs can sometimes reveal health or religious information, for example "halal" or "nut allergy". Only enter what you are comfortable with the couple knowing. It is used only for planning the wedding.

Collected automatically

  • Like most websites, our hosting and database providers keep short-term technical logs, including IP addresses and browser type, to run the service and keep it secure.
  • When you sign in, your browser stores a sign-in token in its local storage so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics.

How we use it

  • To sign hosts in and keep their invitations private to them.
  • To show each guest their own invitation and record their reply.
  • To show hosts their guest list and replies.
  • To keep the service working and secure.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or send marketing emails.

Who can see what

  • Hosts see only their own invitations, guests and replies.
  • A guest sees only their own invitation and reply, through a link containing a long random code. They cannot see the guest list or anyone else's reply. Anyone the link is forwarded to can open it, so hosts and guests should keep links private.
  • Photos added to an invitation can be viewed by anyone who has the photo's web address, because guests need to see them without signing in.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase stores the database, sign-in accounts and photos. Our project is hosted in Singapore.
  • Netlify hosts the website files.
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google" for hosts who choose it, and serves the fonts the site uses. Loading a font tells Google your IP address.
  • jsDelivr serves a code library the site loads.
  • WhatsApp. When a host taps the WhatsApp button, the message goes through WhatsApp from the host's own phone, under WhatsApp's own terms.

These providers may process data in other countries as part of running their services.

Hosts' responsibility for guest details

When you add guests, you are providing their details to us. Please only add people you are genuinely inviting, and only the details needed to invite them.

How long we keep it

  • Invitations, guest lists, replies and photos are kept until the host deletes them. Deleting an invitation also deletes its guests, their replies and its photos.
  • We recommend hosts delete invitations a few months after the wedding, once they no longer need the guest list.
  • Accounts are kept until the host asks us to delete them.

Your choices and rights

  • Hosts can view and edit everything they entered, and delete any invitation, from the dashboard. To delete your account entirely, email us.
  • Guests can change their reply at any time from their link. To have your details removed, ask the couple, or email us with the invitation link and we will delete them.
  • Anyone can ask us what personal information we hold about them, or ask us to correct it. We will reply within 30 days.

If you are in Singapore, the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) gives you these rights. You can also contact the Personal Data Protection Commission if you are not satisfied with our response.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules make sure each host can reach only their own data and each guest only their own reply. No system is perfectly secure, but we will tell affected people promptly if we learn of a breach that affects them.

Children

Hosts must be at least 18. Guests of any age may receive an invitation, but their details are entered and controlled by the host.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will show a notice on the site.
