      Group link

      One link for a group chat, such as old classmates or colleagues. Each person types their name and number when they reply, and is added to the list below.

      Share on WhatsApp Preview

      Anyone who gets this link can reply, even if it's forwarded. Replies from the same WhatsApp number update the earlier reply. Replies that look like someone already on your list are marked possible duplicate.
